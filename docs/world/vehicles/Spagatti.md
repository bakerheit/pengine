# Spagatti

[Vehicle directory](README.md) · [World](../README.md)

Italian-coded exotic grand tourer.

## Manufacturer history and character

Spagatti was founded in Italy in 1950 by a small postwar grand-touring concern.
It sold romance as much as engineering: long drives, expensive lunches, a loud
arrival, and the belief that a fast car can make any owner look more
interesting. The company was never the biggest Italian maker, which is exactly
why it doubled down on presentation.

Its early cars earned attention for low bodies, dramatic proportions, and
interiors that made a modest cabin feel ceremonial. Spagatti's racing history
is intentionally uneven. It has enough old photographs, club trophies, and
half-remembered runs to feed the legend, but not a clean list of championships
that settles the argument.

By the later twentieth century, the company had shifted from small sports cars
to expensive grand tourers. The **Shū** carries that attitude: exotic, polished,
and a little absurd on purpose. Spagatti's joke works best when the car still
looks genuinely desirable. The brand should never become a disposable gag.
