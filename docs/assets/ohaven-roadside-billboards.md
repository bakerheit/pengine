# O'Haven roadside billboards

Two separate double-post boards use the shared 12 x 5.5125 m panel frame and
1280 x 588 artwork:

- TacoMaco stands at `(-500, -1822)`, 32 m off Route 1's Kepler Reach near its
  `(-500, -1790)` road point. Its face points south toward traffic.
- Loom Museum stands at `(1756, 415)`, about 31 m off Route 1 on The Strand.
  Its face points northeast toward traffic.

Their footing elevations are sampled from the active terrain seed when the
world is assembled. The signs are kept apart from the Pinatty billboard pair
and their textures map once over each panel.
