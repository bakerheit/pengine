# Vellum southeast skyline

Two new towers close the open southeast edge of Vellum Row without replacing
the bank, apartments, Tacomaco, or the existing Ashford high-rise run.

- Bellweather Crown is a 33-floor office tower with a narrow steel shaft,
  deep setback, rooftop plant, beacon mast, and 924 live window panes.
- Mariner House is a broader 24-floor residential tower with brick trim,
  roof terraces, planters, and 672 live window panes.

Both parcels occupy the outer block column near local grid east 312. They are
shifted eight metres inward and narrowed to 46 metres so their podiums clear
the avenue sidewalk while their foundations remain on Vellum's 12 metre
plate. The unused nominal east-322 alignment enters Nickel Heights' terrain
feather and was rejected after live corner sampling.

The existing tower integration automatically supplies map footprints,
collision-bearing podiums, clear sidewalk-to-lobby paths, distinct crowns,
and independently lit office or residential windows.

Validation uses `neighborhood_towers_tests`,
`authored_city_layout_tests`, `city_map_tests`, and an on-site bounded render
from southeast Vellum.
