# Five 1991 vehicle concepts for Probable Cause

These began as single-view concept images for choosing future vehicles.
Candidates 1, 2 and 4 are now playable; Ashworth and Regalia remain draft
makers in the current brand roster.

| # | Concept | Role and shape |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [GLM Meridian](01-glm-meridian.png) | Cab-forward seven-seat family minivan with sliding doors. |
| 2 | [Rodeo Switchback](02-rodeo-switchback.png) | Short-wheelbase two-door 4x4 with a removable rear hardtop. |
| 3 | [Ashworth Vale](03-ashworth-vale.png) | Modern executive five-door fastback, separate from Karlsdale's period luxury cars. |
| 4 | [Harrow Hookline](04-harrow-hookline.png) | Medium-duty city recovery truck with boom and winch. |
| 5 | [Regalia Borough](05-regalia-borough.png) | Full-size fleet town car with taxi potential, separate from the stretch limousine. |

Before modeling a selected concept, generate and reconcile front, rear, left,
right, top, front three-quarter, and rear three-quarter reference images. These
concept images are not directional modeling references.

Selected and built: [1 GLM Meridian](../../references/glm_meridian/README.md),
[2 Rodeo Switchback](../../references/rodeo_switchback/README.md), and
[4 Harrow Hookline](../../references/harrow_hookline/README.md). All three have
complete seven-angle reference sets and generated runtime assets. Rebuild the
assets with `python3 tools/make_1991_candidates_assets.py` from the repo root.
