# Pinatty southeast skyline

Five authored towers now close the open southeast edge of Pinatty Row without
replacing the bank, apartments, Tacomaco, or the existing Ashford high-rise
run.

- Bellweather Crown is a 33-floor office tower with a narrow steel shaft,
  deep setback, rooftop plant, beacon mast, and 924 live window panes.
- Mariner House is a broader 24-floor residential tower with brick trim,
  roof terraces, planters, and 672 live window panes.
- Wren Glasshouse adds a compact 23-floor mixed-use shaft with a glazed lantern
  crown.
- Cinder Meridian completes the outer southeast row with a 27-floor brick
  office tower and beacon mast.
- Southpoint Spire anchors the district's southern edge with a 31-floor
  mixed-use shaft and twin roof fins.

The new parcels use the outer block column around local grid east 312. They are
narrowed to 46 metres so their podiums clear the avenue sidewalk. Southpoint is
shifted eight metres east inside its wider end block to stay on a stable support
plane. All three foundations stay within two centimetres of their surveyed
support planes, and the active construction parcels remain untouched.

The existing tower integration automatically supplies map footprints,
collision-bearing podiums, clear sidewalk-to-lobby paths, distinct crowns,
and independently lit office or residential windows.

Validation uses `neighborhood_towers_tests`,
`authored_city_layout_tests`, `city_map_tests`, and an on-site bounded render
from southeast Pinatty.
