# Vellum north extension

Eleventh Street and Twelfth Street continue only the east side of Vellum Row
two blocks past Tenth Street. Juniper, Ashford, Wren, and Cinder reach the new
rows and stop cleanly at Twelfth. The western grid, hospital split, and Vellum
Row arterial keep their original ends, leaving the Ferrone Road fork open and
preserving the district's established silhouette.

Six 54 x 30 m tower parcels sit east of the Vellum Row arterial in the new
three-block-wide rows. They use different heights, materials, uses, setbacks, and crown types
(terraces, mast, lantern, and twin fins), so the addition reads as a skyline
run rather than a repeated prefab wall. The parcels fit on the original Vellum
plate, so the expansion does not move the district boundary or reshape terrain.

`city_roads_tests` verifies both streets and their eight east-side junctions.
`neighborhood_towers_tests` verifies the seventeen tower sites,
their road/terrain clearance, collision-bearing lobby paths, facade panes, and
the thirteen-tower eastern skyline, including the two new outer-frontage towers
at a still-readable 0.15 rad minimum spawn view angle.
