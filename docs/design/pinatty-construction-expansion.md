# Pinatty construction expansion

The Pinatty perimeter construction run now has four distinct active parcels:

- Steel Frame Annex: a twelve-floor open frame, core, tower crane, office, and
  staged stock.
- Concrete Deck Works: a four-level parking/deck shell with columns, ramp,
  formwork, rebar cage, and barriers.
- Facade Retrofit: a retained low-rise shell wrapped in four scaffold levels,
  facade panels, hoist, and site support.
- Demolition Parcel: foundation remnants, broken slabs, rubble, excavator,
  dumpster, dust screen, and controlled street edge.

The Steel Frame Annex and Demolition Parcel sit in the outer Briar Street
column. Their earlier cells were already occupied by Halloway Police Station
and Tacomaco. The outer Cinder Street column was rejected because Nickel
Heights' terrain feather pulls it below Pinatty's 12 m datum. The whole-city
authored-lot audit now guards those separations.

Two additional 54 x 30 m blocks in the northern Mercer Avenue column carry
paired skyscrapers: Twin Towers Block A and Twin Towers Block B. Each has one
shared podium, two separated structural cores, independent glazing/spandrels,
different heights, crowns, and rooftop plant rooms. Their former southern
cells sat up to 0.65 m above their authored ground under Halloway Square's
terrain feather.

All six lots use the same authored grid basis as the existing construction
yard. `construction_expansion_tests` checks road clearance, ground height,
existing-lot separation, site variation, and the two-core-per-shared-block
contract. World collision and map footprints are appended from the same baked
part descriptions used for rendering.
