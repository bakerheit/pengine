# Tidewater Farm

Tidewater Farm occupies the open ground east of Route 1 between Old Tide
Street and the north end of the Apron Spine. Its entrance is a dead-end dirt
spur from Old Tide Street; it does not create an at-grade Route 1 crossing.

The 125 x 160 metre grass parcel contains a terrain-supported farmhouse, an
open red barn, a central packed-earth drive, fourteen alternating green and
ripe crop rows, perimeter fencing, and a water tank. Foundations and crop rows
sample the live terrain instead of flattening the Saltmarsh/Ostend transition.
The full parcel suppresses procedural trees. The map shows the lot, both
buildings, and the two crop blocks.

Validation lives in `tidewater_farm_tests`: authored inventory, parcel bounds,
Old Tide connection, Route 1 clearance, and scatter exclusion. Whole-city lot
separation is covered by `authored_city_layout_tests`; road geometry and drape
remain covered by the existing city-road suites.
