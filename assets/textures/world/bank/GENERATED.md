# Bank interior textures

The base set was generated with OpenAI built-in imagegen on 2026-09-03; the
O'Haven sign and ATM edits were generated on 2026-09-05. Source images remain
in the Codex generated-images folder; runtime PNGs are reduced to their listed
sizes. These are diffuse artwork, not normal or roughness maps.

## terrazzo-floor.png

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production-ready photorealistic game material texture for the enterable Pinatty Savings & Trust bank.
Primary request: A seamless square albedo texture of a polished late-1980s bank lobby terrazzo floor: warm ivory cement, fine restrained cream, charcoal and muted brass stone chips, subtle thin straight brass divider grid defining four equal square tiles. Photorealistic surface detail but diffuse only, no specular highlights, no reflections. Uniform scale. Perfectly orthographic overhead, edge-to-edge, tileable all four edges.
Constraints: one square image, no watermark, no presentation board, no mockup, no border outside the material, no baked directional shadows or lighting gradients.
